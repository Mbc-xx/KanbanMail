Settings

Accounts

Label, icon and color are saved at once. A right click on a badge in the top bar opens them too.

Extra senders

Other mailboxes you can send from, chosen with From when you write an email. Only for sending: their emails are not read here, and the copy of what you send goes to this account's Sent folder. Passwords are kept in the keychain.

Disconnect

Forget this account on this computer and remove the password from the keychain. Your emails stay on the server.

The board columns, left to right. Drag to reorder; each column shows an IMAP folder (choose an existing one, or type a new name to create it).

Waiting for an answer

In this column, an email shows “Replied” when an answer in the same conversation has arrived (click it to open the answer), and how long it has been waiting once it passes the time above.

Still to do

An email in this column shows how many days it has been there once it passes the time above, in red after twice as long; the column title counts them. Pinned emails count too.

Done

Leave the folder empty for Archive, or type a name (“Done archive”) to keep finished work apart: it is created if missing. Days count from when the email arrived in the column; pinned and unread emails stay. It happens while Pigeonhole is open, with Undo.

Conversations

One card per conversation, showing its latest email, the people involved and how many emails it has. Archive, delete, pin and drag act on the whole conversation.

Remote images can tell the sender when and where you opened the email. When off, you can load them for a single email with "Load images".

Signatures

One signature for each mailbox, added to new emails and replies. Changing From in an email swaps it. An extra sender without a signature of its own uses the main one.

This signature has an image linked from the internet (usually because it was pasted from another email app). Many recipients will see a grey box instead of it. Remove it and add the image again with the image button: it will then travel inside your emails.

Write it here or paste one from another email client. Formatting and images are sent as HTML; plain-text clients get the text version.

Sending

Added in blind copy to every new email and reply. You can still change it in each email.

After "Send" the email waits this long with an "Undo" button, which brings it back to the composer. If the app is closed in the meantime, the email stays in Drafts.

Saved replies

Texts written once and put in an email with “Saved replies” while writing, or in a quick reply. Drag to reorder.

Important emails

Important emails come from people you wrote to, your contacts and (optionally) your colleagues; newsletters and automatic notifications are left out. The same choice drives the Important filter, notifications and counts. Your lists below win over everything: one address or @domain per line.

Aliases and old addresses that reach this mailbox, one per line. For the other accounts in the top bar, "only important" counts the unread emails with you in To.

Drag the folders to change their order, choose which ones appear in the sidebar and in the Move to… lists, and give them a color. The color also marks the board column and the folder tags in search results and All Mail.

Rules

New emails arriving in the Inbox are moved by the first matching rule. Matching ignores upper/lower case and accents.

Changes apply right away so you can see them; Save keeps them, Cancel goes back.

Theme and email background are kept for each account, to tell them apart at a glance; size and the rest stay the same for all. Turned off, every account takes the theme chosen here.

Copy on this Mac

Emails are kept on this Mac so they open instantly, can be searched and read without a connection, and changes made offline are sent later. Recent emails of the Inbox and the board columns are downloaded in the background.

Export and import

Save your columns, signature, rules, folders and settings to a file (without the password), to keep a backup or move them to another computer.
